Dear Box Collectors,


I haven't had a lot of time this month to just sit with my body. My classes this quarter have been very demanding. Late in the evenings I tend to be too tired to hold space for myself or think seriously about what I want to make next for TBC society.
I have, however, taken an hour here and there over the past few weeks to fill a few sketchbook pages, scribble and scratch questions and fears in their half formed states. A sort of where's waldo, if waldo did not wear his signature striped jumper and hat but was undoubtedly present.
It has been a good exercise as I have been drawing in really tight narrative/lens/mindset for the last year making mostly watercolor animals.
I think I am still hiding myself in my art. I have been thinking a lot about how one can hide in patterns, as a neurodivergent person, a gender-nonconforming person and as an artist. How patterns can be a level of abstraction from the self that is both comforting and isolating. Sketching, I hold nothing back and chaos reigns. Now, what to make of it...


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
