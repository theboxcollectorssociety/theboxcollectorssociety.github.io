Dear Box Collectors,


I will be taking this January to develop my 2024 goals for The Box Collectors Society, so you probably won't see much from me in the ways of art or social media posts. I know I want my art to include more about nonbinary and trans identity, but I am not yet sure how this will manifest. I have been drawing forever, working with textiles on and off over the past three years and am excited to announce I have recently acquired a punch needle which I think combines the two perfectly. Nothing at all to do with how wonderful the queer textile artist 'community' is and how much I want to be a part of it...
(see Samuel Luke, Niklas Knits, Maker Bee, Jackass Collective, just to name a few)
In 2022 I did a small fundraiser for Lyric in San Francisco, an LGBTQIA+ youth center where I made cookies and collaborated with another queer artist to create stickers and a zine to complete a giftbox for which all proceeds were donated.
I would like to make something like this an annual thing. You'll be the first to know when I get this organized.


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
