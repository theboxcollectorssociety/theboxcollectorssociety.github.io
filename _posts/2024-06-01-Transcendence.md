Dear Box Collectors,


I went to see the beautiful Crooners: a Lavender Live-Sing Cabaret at Stookey's Blue Room this past Sunday with my partner. We got all dressed up, and I should have taken a picture! The performers gave me goosebumps, and I smiled enough that my cheeks hurt the whole drive home. It is a monthly event, so if you are looking for a date night in June, keep an eye out here. Tickets were $30 each.
We really wanted to go camping for the long weekend, but we waited too long and couldn't find any site within two hours. I am really hoping to go this coming month and look forward to sharing our trip with you.
Lastly if you are local and know anyone running a small pride event who needs a volunteer, please send me a dm on Instagram @theboxcollectorssoc!
Happy Pride!


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,
