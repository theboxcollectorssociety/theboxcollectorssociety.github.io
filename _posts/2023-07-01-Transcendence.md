Dear Box Collectors,


I am eight magnificent weeks post-op, and this weekend I am going camping for the first time since my surgery in May. For those of you who don't know, I love camping.
Before I knew what gender dysphoria was and way before I knew I was trans, being in my tent was one of my only true happy places, with being in the woods a close second, only surpassed by being in my art.
When I had tough days, I would remind myself how I always had this happy place to go to, even if I couldn't go until the weekend or next week or next month.
For years I thought I was in pursuit of finding this happy place in my day to day. I just needed to assemble all of the ingredients, and it would rise, like a souffle, in my apartment, in my relationships.
I am realizing that much like cooking, you can make happiness a lot of different ways. As I continue to untangle my discomfort and tend to my body, I look forward, with a new flicker of confidence, to creating a long list, starting with my tent of course, of things that bring me happiness.


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
