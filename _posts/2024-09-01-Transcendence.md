Dear Box Collectors,


This month has been a lot of figuring out body stuff unrelated to gender. I'm trying to get to the bottom of a chronic tummy ache situation (aren't we all though 🙄) and have been in an out of the doc's a lot getting a variety of tests. So far my organs seem ok. I'm very thankful, but I am in a lot of daily discomfort, so am hoping to find ways to manage that soon.
In the meantime I finished my first month long sketchbook challenge hosted by Eliza of @doolittleillustrations, and I am really proud of myself! Through a combination of traditional gouache and colored pencil artworks AND digital artworks I was able to keep up with the pace, finishing a 3.5" x 5.5" piece every other day.
It has been so delicious getting back into drawing from photographs. I would really like to plan a large canvas painting to work on this fall, so I am on the hunt for the perfect photo, something cozy and just for me, that my partner and I can hang in our home. I'll let you know when I find it!
Recently I signed up to take a 4-day ceramics class at PLACE in Oakland. I am really looking forward to it and will be sure to share more details and hopefully some photos next month.
Lastly, I am aware that WIX is on the BDS boycott list, and I wanted to let you know that I am taking steps to move The Box Collectors Society website and newsletter to a new platform.


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
