Dear Box Collectors,


My partner and I have moved to a new apartment. It gets really lovely sunlight in the morning and it is quiet at night, two rare treats for city living.
I also had my first experience where I felt my name was a barrier to being seen as my actual gender identity. To be honest, I still really like my name. I like that it is symmetrical, and I like all of the soft, rounded letters bumping elbows. But in this moment, I realized just how much other people rely on names as signifiers for gender identity in our culture.
I think it is possible to appear in such a way that no matter what your name is people are unsure of your gender, ask your pronouns, or assume you use they/them pronouns, but I haven't found it yet.
It is a real challenge. I feel I have to prepare both how I look and how I will cope with situations where someone misgenders me, asks invasive questions, or stares maliciously, all of which happen every week.
At the end of the day, I know I just have to take it slow. Try things in safe spaces, which is now mostly just at home and surround myself with people that understand without explanation. To the friends I do have who just get it and my partner who is hands down my biggest cheerleader, thank you, thank you, thank you.


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
