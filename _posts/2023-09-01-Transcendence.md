Dear Box Collectors,


I am 4 months post-op, and I still struggle to describe how sometimes I am overcome with understanding for myself, maybe even with the mightiest of words, love for myself, and my strength, while merely brushing my teeth, while tying my shoes. I think often about how many people grow up with a sense of innate confidence, this seedling that I now hold in my palm.
I self identify as neurodivergent, and I am playing with ways to describe my experience creatively. My exploration of gender and how I move through the world have been hand and hand. While I was learning to understand my chest dysphoria, I started to notice other feelings of discomfort in my body.
I feel like my skin is a thin, fragile membrane between my guts and the air around me. There are days where the air itself feels abrasive, like I am moving through a delicate cloud of super fine needles. Other days it feels like viscous sap.
On good days it feels like I am riding an unraveling thread. I am frictionless, fluid movement following beautiful, predictable, mathematical curvature.


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
