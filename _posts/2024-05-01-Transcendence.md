Dear Box Collectors,


Since the beginning of the year I have been in a sort of art limbo, a writhing spaghetti monster of feelings with unclear vision (I mean you try to see with noodle curtain bangs, it is hard!). But things are slowing getting teased apart. Returning to my sketchbook has helped.
I use my sketchbook to make art that I know I will share online, but I also use it like a drawing diary and many of those pages I do not share. This divide between the art I make and the art I present publicly is something I am still sitting with.
I have been asking myself "what?" for several months now, as in "what am I trying to say?" Now I am starting to ask myself "what?" as in "what do I fear?" --and boy is that a juicy question for making art. I can draw for hours just trying to dodge that one.
Drawing has always been something I do to feel safe and regulated, but it is shifting. My sketchbook isn't just somewhere I go to escape anymore, it is somewhere I go to seek understanding. And I am finding it.
It is an incredible feeling when you discover something about yourself through a piece you're working on. In my brain I am a paleontologist. Dust hangs in the hot summer air, the taste of salt is on my lips, and with a delicate flick of my wrist my brush reveals just a glimpse of the biggest, oldest, uncategorized phalange the world has ever seen. How could I stop now?


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
