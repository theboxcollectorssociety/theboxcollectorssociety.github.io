Dear Box Collectors,

If you think this newsletter looks different, it is because it does! Wix is on the BDS boycott list,\ 
and so I have been working hard this past month to shift all of my content off of that platform.\ 
After researching alternatives, I decided to go the DIY route. As long as I have a small readership,\ 
I will be using gmail for newsletters. Additionally, I am coding my own website via GitHub pages.\ 
I am really liking the simplicity of this set up, and I hope it appeals to you. I will note, some\ 
features do not yet work on mobile.
I started The Box Collectors Society to explore gender and creativity openly and to connect with\
folx like you. Although I would love to sell my art (who doesn't want to feel like people love their\
work and that being an artist is a valid career choice), that is not my main focus. Mostly I just \
want to share interesting things I read, interesting art I find and see how my own art practice \
develops over time.\

As for a personal gender update, I recently started taking photographs of myself. I know "fit" or \
"ootd" photos are nothing new and can feel entitled or oblivious when in our feeds next to current \
events, but I won't be posting them anytime soon. These are just for me.
I went a long time avoiding full-body photos of myself, avoiding photos entirely or only taking \
profiles from the shoulders up. When I saw myself in photos, I could recognize my face, but it felt\
like it was cut and pasted onto someone else's body. It was not that I hated the body or wished it \
were different in specific ways, I just didn't recognize it. This may not make sense if you haven't \
experienced gender dysphoria, but I think it is a pretty common experience.\
The goal of this daily photo is two-fold. It is in part to figure out what clothes make my body look\
like I feel on the inside, for example like what clothes create proportions that are affirming and \
generally more masculine. I'm seeking congruence, not fashion or followers. I also am trying to \
reshape how I see myself, to unlearn gender performance and just be myself. Documenting this process\
is a way of validating my experience in the privacy and safety of my little home.\
Lastly, it is October which means it is time for Inktober! I will be posting daily to my Instagram\
with a new drawing following the classic Inktober prompts as well as a reel following the Peachtober \
prompts. If you don't know, Peachtober is hosted by artist Sha'an d'Anthes of [@furrylittlepeach](https://www.instagram.com/furrylittlepeach/). Her\
work is awesome, and you should definitely check it out.\
I realize this is a lot of drawing! I am keeping it simple though, and I think I can swing it. I also\
am not too worried if I miss a day or two. My main goal is to draw daily and just keep exploring\
different art styles until I find the one that feels most *me*.\

As always, thanks for reading.\

xoxo,
Hannah\
