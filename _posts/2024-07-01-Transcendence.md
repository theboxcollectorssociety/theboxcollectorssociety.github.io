Dear Box Collectors,


This month I have been overwhelmed with coursework, so I don't have a whole lot to report. For those of you who do not know, I returned to school a couple of years ago to study software engineering. More on why I chose to do this another time, but it was greatly driven by my social and sensory needs. This month I have been out-of-body more than I would like, but I only have another week or so to go in this quarter, so I am pushing through.
I have returned to my planner I used when I was working two jobs, baking and teaching art at an after school center. At the time I had to literally schedule my days by the hour to find time for art. This planner helps A LOT with my anxiety, but I know days this busy are not sustainable for me, and I am really feeling the accumulated exhaustion start to affect my motivation to do, well, anything.
In the realm of gender, I will say this year's pride was quite special as it was my first year post top surgery where I felt healed and confident enough to be shirtless. It was very empowering. To feel like I could go to pride as myself, that I didn't need to dress for sex or wear my identities as flags or pins.
On Friday I went to the San Francisco Trans March and the El Rio afterparty which was a fundraiser for TGIJP's re-entry programming for currently and formerly incarcerated TGI (transgender, gender-variant and intersex people) people.
And, on Saturday my partner and I went to Dolores Park for a few hours to soak up some sun, eat some grocery store sushi, do some drawing, and of course see some 🍑's.


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
