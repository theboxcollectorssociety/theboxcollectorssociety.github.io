Dear Box Collectors,


The last couple of weeks I have been keeping busy in between classes. I completed my first sketchbook challenge, created my first vlog, took the W3C's free Web Accessibility course, and started working on a new programming project -- a memory card game that can be played with text, images, voiceover and/or braille keyboard.
Busy does not always mean thriving for me, and so I must say I have had an extraordinary amount of gender dysphoria this month. All my clothes are uncomfortable, and I refuse to buy anything new or thrifted because I don't have a clear vision of what I need (if anything). Pants, long shorts and large t-shirts all feel pretty good, but don't seem to tell others anything about my gender, and this is where I linger.
It is so tempting to perform gender to influence how people perceive you. If I wore the plaid button ups and chinos, I might get gendered differently. For that matter, if my hair wasn't blue, I might get gendered differently. People at the grocery might use they/them or he/him pronouns or even just pause, uncertain, before defaulting to she/her. But it wouldn't feel like I was being me on the outside. I don't know the answer yet, but I do know I am proud to be trans and nonbinary in such a way I've never really been proud of myself before.
On a different note, I've also been really struggling to meet folx locally. I struggle to attend events when I don't feel confident. Additionally, the Venn diagram between gender non-conforming folx that want to make art together, have time to plan things and have access to low-cost or no-cost community spaces and supplies is, well, very small. I did have a really positive experience making a sketchbook at a bookbinding class hosted by Rock Paper Scissors Collective in Oakland at the beginning of the month and plan to attend more of their events in the near future.
Lastly, I will be participating in Eliza Williams @doolittleillustrations sketchbook challenge for all of August, so be sure to check out The Box Collectors Society on Instagram to see my mixed media/gouache illustrations.


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
