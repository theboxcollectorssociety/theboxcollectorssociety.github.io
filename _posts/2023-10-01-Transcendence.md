Dear Box Collectors,


In September, I had my first market, and I wore my first suit to a wedding, both of which I have been dreaming of for many years. October holds even more excitement.
There is Halloween of course. This will be my first year getting to dress up post-op, and I am looking forward to trying on costumes. In previous years I have felt pressured to choose between leotards and dresses. Black cat to corpse bride, I've been them all. I am looking forward to finding a costume that fits me comfortably and shows off my creativity instead of my body. I don't feel like I need to perform a gender like I used to. Undead school girls and werewolves have a history steeped in the gender binary. But what about ghouls, what about monsters? Do they have a gender?
I am curious to start exploring the dark and ugly sides of my imagination. I think up until recently I was afraid that if I strayed away from positivity, that I would be consumed by unhappiness. But I was already unhappy, and it was the very crumbling of this defense that allowed me to start healing in ways unanticipated. I have an increasingly firm grasp of the good, bad and the ugly, and I interested in letting it sink into my art. I feel the ground shifting beneath my art practice and excited to see what crawls up between the cracks.
I think it would have taken me a lot longer to look inward had I not met my wonderful partner who seems to have a supernatural ability to reach into the deepest, darkest places of my mind and empower me. I am thrilled to announce the second and most exciting thing about this October, we are to be married.


As always, thanks for reading.


xoxo,

Hannah
