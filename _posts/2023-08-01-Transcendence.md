Dear Box Collectors,


I am 13 weeks post-op. I can sleep on my side again (finally). My scars are smooth and flat and vary in color from bubblegum pink to raspberry red. I love them.
When I stopped shaving my legs nine years ago, I imagined I was a cricket who could conjure song between shin and calf. Recently I started rowing at home. When I start I spawn in a small photograph of a river I have taped to the wall. A frog, a salamander, a snake, a salmon, growing, morphing from creature to creature, breathing deeply, as sinews shift. So this is how it feels to move with ease, to move without thinking about moving.
Otherwise, I am doing calculus 4+ hours a day. I have nine more days in calc 3 and let me tell you, math has made me believe in myself in ways I never thought I would. It has, no pun intended, become an integral part of my gender journey as I find my confidence.
I have applied for a booth at a craft fair, so keep an eye out on the gram. I'll post if I get in for the September show!


As always, thanks for reading.

xoxo,

Hannah
