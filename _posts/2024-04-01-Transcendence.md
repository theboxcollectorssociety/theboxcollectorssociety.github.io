Dear Box Collectors,


As we roll into another month, I am still doing a lot of drawing. I am deep in the woods sitting with the question "what?"-- what am I trying to say? I am listening for myself, and it is going to take awhile. Drawing, like cheese to a mouse, like meat to a wild beast, learning to trust.
I painted this weekend for the first time in months. I'm a working on a piece I think will fit nicely into a second installment of my compartmentalize me series from my art residency at Arts Letters & Numbers in 2019. This series originally explored a couple ideas, one being the idea of storing emotions and memories in my flesh and two the idea of being able to feel sounds and feelings resonating in my body.
In my current painting, I am still using a palette that is fleshy, but it has a black background as opposed to a white one. I think in the first part of the series I was much more interested in exposing what was within, this idea of letting light in, opening things up and looking and showing. This current piece is more about exploring within my body, thus dark, red and glistening.

As always, thanks for reading.
